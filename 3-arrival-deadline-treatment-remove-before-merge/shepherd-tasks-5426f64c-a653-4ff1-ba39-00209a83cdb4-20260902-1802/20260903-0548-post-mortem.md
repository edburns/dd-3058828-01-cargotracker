## Section 8: Observations and Recommendations

### 8.1 What worked well

- Full completion: all five tasks merged to the required non-default base branch.
- Strong phase boundaries: stage 30 consistently stopped before Ready for review, and stage 40 completed review and merge.
- Review convergence: every task ended with a finding-free review on the final head.
- Recovery: the [#6](https://github.com/edburns/dd-3058828-01-cargotracker/issues/6) session resumed after a long gap without losing campaign state.
- Measured telemetry: all ten sessions captured result, usage-checkpoint, and OTEL artifacts.

### 8.2 What did not work well

- One inactive interval dominated total elapsed time while serial execution blocked all later tasks.
- Campaign lesson publication repeatedly conflicted with the issue-required section format.
- The local campaign lesson snapshot does not reflect the lessons reported as merged.
- Token values were redacted even though token attribute names were emitted, preventing token-efficiency analysis.
- The run lacked a parent-level job log that could have correlated host/script activity with the long session gap.

### 8.3 Recommendations

1. Add explicit heartbeat and wait-state records to stage 40, including review request time, last poll time, host resume detection, and notification delivery time. Classify long gaps separately from remote review latency.
2. Add a configurable inactivity ceiling and resumable checkpoint around review waits so a suspended session can restart deterministically without holding the serial campaign indefinitely.
3. Reconcile the lesson contract: either require issues to permit conversion from candidate to validated headings, or preserve the required candidate heading until after merge and publish validated lessons in a separate campaign-base update.
4. Validate lesson-section naming and preservation locally before requesting the post-publication CCRA review. This would have prevented at least five finding-bearing rounds across the campaign.
5. Refresh or separately export `campaign-lessons.md` from `experiment/shepherd-treatment` after every merge so the campaign metadata directory remains an authoritative local artifact.
6. Always capture a top-level `YYYYMMDD-HHMM-job-logs.txt` for the stage-25 driver, including task boundaries, process lifecycle, wait starts/completions, and final exit status.
7. Preserve unredacted aggregate input/output token counters when policy permits. If raw token fields must remain redacted, emit a non-sensitive terminal aggregate alongside the usage checkpoint.
8. Consider limited parallelism for independent task preparation, while retaining ordered merges and rebasing later work against the campaign base. At minimum, do not let a review-wait inactivity interval prevent safe preparation of the next task.
