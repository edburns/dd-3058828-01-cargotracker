The resolved facade contract is `void changeDeadline(String trackingId, Date arrivalDeadline)`. `DefaultBookingServiceFacade` converts only the identifier with `new TrackingId(trackingId)` and passes the same `Date` to the application service. The aggregate mutation belongs exclusively to `BookingService`: the facade must not load a cargo, access a repository, parse formatted dates, or expose domain types. No new chronological deadline rule is permitted.

Research established that Open Liberty packaging compiles tests but the historical default skips Arquillian execution; a focused container-free test may use a hand-written fake, but no mocking dependency or test-runtime modernization is justified.
